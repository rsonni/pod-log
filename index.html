Every game we've logged, split by pod size — because a seat at a three-player table is worth a third of the wins, and a seat at a five-player table is worth a fifth. Comparing them without splitting them hides more than it shows.

The table

Share of the wins taken by each seat, reading clockwise from the player who goes first. Gold means the seat is taking more than chance would hand it. Hover a seat for its own win rate.

Standings

Expected is how many wins pure chance owed them: a third of each three-player game, a quarter of each four-player one, added up. Gap is actual wins minus that, in wins. Avg seat is where in turn order they tended to sit, and roll win % is how often that was seat one.

    Nothing on this page talks to the internet on its own. Refresh card data looks the commanders up on Scryfall once, on demand. Save updated copy then writes a fresh file with those results and your current log baked in, so the copy you share never calls out at all.

    Format is date | deck(seat) vs deck(seat) … | winner | how it ended | knocked out, in order. The last field is optional and unlocks the survival numbers — list the losers first-out first and leave the winner off. Add a line, press update, and every number on this page moves. Pods of any size work; a fifth name makes it a five-player table on its own.

    Commander names link to Scryfall, which also supplies the card images on hover. A dotted underline with a dot beside it means the shorthand matched more than one card and the link falls back to a search — pin the exact name in CARD_OVERRIDES to fix it.
